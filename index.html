Hidden Blackland Prairie Explorer

Where could never-plowed prairie still survive in the Texas Blackland belt?

The Texas Blackland Prairie once covered ~43,000 km². Only a small fraction (often cited as under 1%) is thought to survive unplowed. This app maps the candidate search space for surviving remnants: Vertisol clay soils (Usterts) that are grassland or pasture today.

Users: TPWD and conservation groups, landowners, researchers.

1 · Then and now (1951)

USDA 1951 index mosaic, Collin County (TxGIO). Positional offset of several metres is possible. Occurrence polygons (TPWD TXNDD) are restricted to signed-in UTD users.

2 · Measure an area

Draw a polygon or rectangle on the map to summarise candidate prairie inside it.

3 · Prioritise patches

Priority score (0–100) = 40% patch size + 40% closeness to a known remnant + 20% compactness. Move the handles to show only the patches you care about.

4 · Label patches on the 1951 photo

For researchers (UTD sign-in). Patches in Collin County are shown in random order; decide whether each looked plowed in 1951. Known occurrences are hidden while labelling so the call stays blind.
